No third-party code is included. The USDA parser, the USDC reader and writer, the LZ4 and integer decoders, the USDZ reader, the composition, the 3D renderer and the exporters were written for this extension from the published USD file formats. The sample files in `samples/` were written for this extension; `sample-robot.usdc` and `sample-robot.usdz` were made from `sample-robot.usda` and `sample-parts.usda` with the USD command line tools.
